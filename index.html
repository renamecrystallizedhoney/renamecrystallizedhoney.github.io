A Naming Campaign

"Crystallized honey" Call it Sodatanite. (pronounced So-duh-tuh-night)

Honey that has crystallized is a real mineral-like formation with its own structure, texture, and behavior. It deserves a real name — not a two-word description bolted onto a food label.

Why it needs a new name

Two words were never going to hold up

"Crystallized honey" describes a process, not a substance. It's the equivalent of calling a diamond "compressed carbon" every time you mention it. The formation has consistent structure, its own texture profile, and predictable behavior — the hallmarks of something that earns its own name in any other field.
